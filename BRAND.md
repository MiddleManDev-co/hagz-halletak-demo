# MATRAH brand system

## Brand idea

**MATRAH | مَطرح** is the trusted place to discover a venue that fits the event and is credible for the required date.

Primary promise:

> The right venue, on the right date.<br>
> المطرح الصح، في المعاد الصح.

## Logo

The mark combines three product ideas:

- the arch is a venue entrance and a welcoming place
- the clay dot is the event date the customer is trying to secure
- the brass arc is a 360-degree view and the connection between customer and venue

The production mark is [`public/matrah-mark.svg`](public/matrah-mark.svg). It is intentionally flat, compact, and readable at app-icon and navigation sizes.

| Matrah clay | `burgundy` | `#BD5439` |
| Deep clay | `burgundy-2` | `#943B2A` |
| Brass | `gold` | `#A77E36` |

Teal communicates operational trust and availability. Clay carries the warmth of important occasions. Ivory and brass keep the product premium without wedding-template ornament.

## Typography

Alexandria is the single Arabic and Latin product family. It keeps the bilingual identity consistent and avoids a visibly different English product.
